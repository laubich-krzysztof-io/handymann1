# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: owners and managers of better guesthouses, small hotels (incl. 4-star), aparthotels and investment apartments in the Karkonosze, often owners who live outside the region and need someone trustworthy on site. Secondary: businesses and private homes. They come to the site when something broke or when they want a property looked after; they want to know who will come, what it costs and that the property is in good hands.

## Product Purpose
handyman.karkonosze is one craftsman's repair and property-care service: plumbing, electrics (SEP licence up to 1 kV), installation and small renovations, plus regular property care (walk-rounds on a set rhythm, a defect list after each visit, one phone number in season). Success: an owner reports a fault or asks about regular care by phone, WhatsApp or the form.

## Positioning
The same person every time, who knows the property; faults are logged and fixed before guests notice; transparent published rates (net, gross with 23% VAT) and a worked sample bill. Within 15 km of Jelenia Góra (straight line), plus all of Karpacz, Kowary and Szklarska Poręba; no travel beyond that.

## Operating Context
Season-driven hospitality; reports arrive by phone, WhatsApp (often with a photo) or the site form, which hands the message to WhatsApp or e-mail. Quote from a photo, exact quote on site, invoice with labour and materials listed separately. Polish-only site and service (German and English versions removed on the user's request, 2026-10-28).

## Capabilities and Constraints
Astro static site on Vercel, Polish only: home at / and privacy page at /prywatnosc/ (old /pl/, /de/, /en/ addresses redirect to /). Prices live in src/config.ts (same numbers in the Dyspozytor repo, lib/pricing.ts): first hour incl. travel in zone 1 200 zł net, each further started 30 min 70 zł net, travel to zone 2 +40 zł net per visit (no percentage surcharges on travel), parts at cost (time spent buying parts during a job counts as work time), inspection without repair priced as the first hour, urgent same day +50%, nights 22–6 and holidays +100%, Saturdays and Sundays in daytime without surcharge, care from 590 zł net/month (4 h, travel in both zones included, response within 24 h incl. weekends, extra work 70 zł per started 30 min without the first-hour fee, unused hours do not roll over). Prices revised 2026-10-28 after a regional price check, on the user's decision. Service area: up to 30 km from Jelenia Góra in two travel zones named by municipality: zone 1 = Jelenia Góra (with Cieplice and Sobieszów) and the gminas Podgórzyn, Mysłakowice, Piechowice, Jeżów Sudecki and Stara Kamienica; zone 2 = Karpacz, Kowary, Szklarska Poręba, Świeradów-Zdrój, Janowice Wielkie, Lwówek Śląski, Kamienna Góra and other places up to 30 km. Domain not bought yet; site stays noindex until launch. Company name, NIP and address still open.

## Brand Commitments
Name and wordmark "handyman.karkonosze" in lowercase. The logo mark (tree, house, stethoscope) stays. Śnieżka with the observatory ("Spodek") stays as the mountain motif, may be drawn differently. Avoid black-with-gold "luxury". Copy addresses clients formally ("Państwo"); the service is called "serwis" in page copy, and "złota rączka" appears only in the search title (user decision, 2026-10-28). The owner's name and portrait stay off the site for now (user decision, site in progress). Quiet, not AI-slop. Green palette is not binding for the premium variant. Premium variant palette: light "Len i świerk" (linen, stone and frost backgrounds, ink text, spruce for buttons, links and the footer), chosen by the user on 2026-10-28.

## Evidence on Hand
Photos of one real job (mountain mural, public/img/mural-*). Header landscape layers and Śnieżka image (public/img). No testimonials, no client logos, no photos of plumbing/electrical work, no portrait yet: none may be invented. Property-log entries and the sample bill are labelled examples.

## Product Principles
- Trust through specifics: real prices, real process, the same person.
- Calm over hype: the owner should feel the property is in good hands.
- Every contact path one tap away, especially on phones.
- Honest labelling of anything illustrative.
